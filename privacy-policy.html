3. Categories of personal data we process

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process the following categories (not every user provides every category):

  • Identity & account data: user ID, username, display name, avatar/photo URL, locale/language, profile bio, referral codes, account status (including bans/moderation flags).
  • Telegram data: Telegram user ID and related Mini App auth payload fields provided by Telegram (for example username, first/last name, language code, photo URL) when you authorize via Telegram.
  • Email & messaging: email address (if you register or verify by email), notification preferences, support ticket content.
  • Wallet & on-chain identifiers: public wallet addresses (e.g. TON / EVM) that you connect; transaction references related to deposits/withdrawals on supported networks.
  • Market activity: positions, stakes, payouts, Free Position / promotional balances, Predict Coin (PRDC) balances and ledger events, comments, reactions, ratings, referrals, and related timestamps.
  • Payment & compliance data: payment status metadata from processors (we do not store full payment card PAN/CVV on Winion servers when cards are processed by a third-party PSP); withdrawal destinations; anti-fraud signals.
  • Device & technical data: IP address, user-agent, device/app type, approximate location derived from IP, cookies or similar identifiers, log files, crash/diagnostic data, and security events.
  • Communications: messages you send to support, in-app messenger content where applicable, and records of pushes/emails/Telegram notifications we send.
  • Social / advertising integrations: identifiers and tokens needed if you connect or authorize Meta (Facebook / Instagram / Threads), X (Twitter), or similar platforms for login, publishing, or measurement, subject to your permissions on those platforms.
